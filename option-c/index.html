Before you come

Do I need an appointment? No. Walk in any day from 7:30 am; we close at 8 pm, and at 6 pm on Sundays. For a home visit, message us a day ahead.

How long should I fast? 8 to 10 hours for fasting sugar, 10 to 12 for a lipid profile or full body check. Plain water is fine. Take your regular medicines unless your doctor says otherwise.

When will I get my report? Most routine tests the same evening, as a PDF on WhatsApp. Vitamins and special tests can take one to two days.

Is the lab NABL approved? Yes. Samples collected at Saifee Lab are processed at a NABL-approved laboratory.

Do I need a prescription? Not for routine tests. If you have one, bring it so we run exactly what your doctor asked for.

How can I pay? Cash or UPI at the centre, or to the phlebotomist on a home visit.

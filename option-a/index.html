Before you come

Do I need an appointment?

No. Walk in any day from 7:30 am; we close at 8 pm, and at 6 pm on Sundays. For a home visit, message us a day ahead.

How long should I fast?

8 to 10 hours for fasting sugar, 10 to 12 hours for a lipid profile or full body check. Plain water is fine. Take your regular medicines unless your doctor says otherwise.

When will I get my report?

Most routine tests the same evening, as a PDF on WhatsApp. Special tests such as vitamins can take one to two days.

Do you come home?

Yes, anywhere in Mumbai. Early-morning visits are the best for fasting tests. Send your area and preferred time on WhatsApp.

Is the lab NABL approved?

Yes. Samples collected at Saifee Lab are processed at a NABL-approved laboratory.

Do I need a doctor's prescription?

Not for routine tests. If you have one, bring it so we run exactly what your doctor asked for.

How can I pay?

Cash or UPI at the centre, or to the phlebotomist on a home visit.

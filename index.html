Frequently asked questions

Before your visit

Do I need an appointment?
No. Walk in any day from 7:30 am. We close at 8 pm (6 pm on Sundays). For home collection, please book a day ahead.
How long should I fast?
8–10 hours for fasting sugar and 10–12 hours for a lipid profile or full body check. Plain water is fine. Continue your regular medicines unless your doctor advises otherwise.
When will I get my report?
Most routine reports the same day, sent as a PDF on WhatsApp. Specialised tests such as vitamins may take 24–48 hours.
Is home collection available in my area?
We collect across Mumbai, including Marol, Saki Naka, Chakala, Andheri, JB Nagar and Powai. Message us your area to confirm a slot.
Do I need a doctor's prescription?
Not for routine tests. If you have one, bring it along so we run exactly what your doctor requested.
How can I pay?
Cash or UPI at the centre, or to our phlebotomist during a home visit.
